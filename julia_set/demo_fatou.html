Zero Condensation & Potential Theory

The equilibrium measure $\mu_J$ on a Julia set governs the limit distribution of zeros of orthogonal polynomials $P_n(z)$. As $n \to \infty$, the roots of $P_n(z) = 0$ condense directly onto $J(f)$.

Fatou Classification Metrics

  • Attracting Basin: $f^k(z) \to z_0$ with $|f'(z_0)| < 1$.
  • Siegel Disk: $f'(z_0) = e^{2\pi i \theta}$ for irrational $\theta$.
  • Parabolic Petal: $f'(z_0) = 1$, slow algebraic convergence.
  • Escaping Basin: Points where $|f^k(z)| \to \infty$.
